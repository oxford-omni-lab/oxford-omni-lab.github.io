About Me

I am a visiting student at the Oxford Machine Learning in NeuroImaging (OMNI) Lab, supervised by Dr. Ana IL Namburete. I am currently completing a Mathematics and Computer Science degree at Haverford College, and am enrolled in an accelerated Master’s degree in Computer and Information Science at the University of Pennsylvania.

My research focuses on automated segmentation of fetal subcutaneous adipose tissue in limb ultrasound images, with the aim of enabling automated fat volume calculation as a biomarker of fetal growth.

Educational Background

I am completing my undergraduate degree in Mathematics and Computer Science at Haverford College, Pennsylvania, USA. During my study abroad year at Pembroke College, Oxford, I joined the OMNI Lab to pursue research in medical image analysis.

Personal Interests

Outside of research, I enjoy dancing, hiking, and travelling.
