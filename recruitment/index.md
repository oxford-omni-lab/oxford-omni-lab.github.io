## DPhil students

Ana supervises through two EPSRC Centres for Doctoral Training: [Healthcare Data Science (HDS)](https://www.bdi.ox.ac.uk/study/cdt) and [Autonomous Intelligent Machines and Systems (AIMS)](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/autonomous-intelligent-machines-and-systems). We look for backgrounds in deep learning, programming or brain image analysis. If you are interested in a rotation in the lab, please contact Ana using the details on her [departmental profile page](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/ana.namburete/).

## Postdoctoral researchers

We have more data and research ideas than postdocs. We offer a collaborative, supportive environment and mentorship towards an independent research career in machine learning and brain image analysis.

## How to apply

1. **Find your theme.** Read about our [research themes]({{ "research" | relative_url }}) and pick the problem that fits your skills.
2. **Check the route.** For a DPhil, check the CDT application deadlines. For a postdoc, think about fellowships too.
3. **Get in touch.** Contact Ana via her [departmental profile](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/ana.namburete/), with your CV, the theme you’re interested in, and a few lines on why.
