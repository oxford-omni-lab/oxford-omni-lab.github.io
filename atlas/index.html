    Growth curves

    Normative growth of 28 brain measures from 18 to 27 weeks' gestation (structure and lobe volumes, cortical surface area, depth and thickness, and Sylvian fissure depth), from 4,205 3D ultrasound scans in the INTERGROWTH-21st study (Wyburd et al., 2026). The band shows the 3rd to 97th centile and the line the median.

    Download the table (CSV)
    Compare a measurement
    Before comparing your own measurements. These growth models were learned from a coordinated multi-site study (INTERGROWTH-21st), in which every scan was acquired with the same protocol and the same ultrasound machine (Philips HD9). Scans from other machines, probes or acquisition protocols, or structures measured with other segmentation methods, may differ systematically from these curves, so interpret any comparison with caution.

    Measures are for one hemisphere (the side further from the probe), except total brain and cavum septum pellucidum. Curves are from the published equations (Supplementary Table 8). For academic research use only; not a clinical tool and not for commercial use.

    Label and measure new scans

    The OMNI ultrasound toolkit runs the same pipeline used in the paper: align a 3D scan to the atlas, label the brain structures, compute volumes and compare them with the growth curves.
