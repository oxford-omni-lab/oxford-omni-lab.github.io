<h2>The problem</h2>
<p>A single measurement means little on its own. To spot a brain that is developing differently, we need to know how healthy brains grow across gestation, and to understand how prenatal brain development relates to neurodevelopment after birth.</p>

<h2>Our approach</h2>
<ol class="omni-steps">
  <li><strong>Measure</strong>Extract brain structure volumes and maturation features from each scan.</li>
  <li><strong>Model growth</strong>Normative growth trajectories by gestational age, from large international cohorts.</li>
  <li><strong>Link to outcomes</strong>Relate prenatal brain features to neurodevelopment at 2 years.</li>
  <li><strong>Evaluate</strong>Work with clinical partners to assess the measures on clinical data.</li>
</ol>
